# 自由主義經典人物精選語錄

---

### 一、約翰·斯圖亞特·密爾  
**John Stuart Mill**

#### 傷害原則  
**The Harm Principle**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">The only purpose for which power can be rightfully exercised over any member of a civilized community, against his will, is to prevent harm to others. Over himself, over his own body and mind, the individual is sovereign.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">對文明社會的任何成員，違背其意志而正當行使權力的唯一目的，就是防止傷害他人。在他自己的身體與心靈之上，個人是最高主權者。</span>

#### 多數人的暴政  
**The Tyranny of the Majority**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">The will of the people, practically means the will of the most numerous or the most active part of the people; the majority, or those who succeed in making themselves accepted as the majority; the people, consequently, may desire to oppress a part of their number; and precautions are as much needed against this as against any other abuse of power.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">所謂人民的意志，實際上不過是人民中人數最多或最積極那部分人的意志；多數人，或那些成功使自己被接受為多數的人，可能渴望壓迫他們中的一部分人；因此，對此採取防範措施，就像防範任何其他權力濫用一樣必要。</span>

#### 不滿足的人勝過滿足的豬  
**Better to be Socrates Dissatisfied**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">It is better to be a human being dissatisfied than a pig satisfied; better to be Socrates dissatisfied than a fool satisfied.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">作一個不滿足的人，勝過一隻滿足的豬；作一個不滿足的蘇格拉底，勝過作一個滿足的傻子。</span>

### 二、托克維爾  
**Alexis de Tocqueville**

#### 多數人的暴政  
**Tyranny of the Majority**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">I know of no country in which there is so little independence of mind and real freedom of discussion as in America. In America the majority raises formidable barriers around the liberty of opinion; within these barriers an author may write what he pleases, but woe to him if he goes beyond them.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">我不知道有哪個國家的思想獨立與真正的討論自由，會像在美國那樣稀少。在美國，多數人在意見自由周圍豎起了強大的屏障；在這些屏障之內，作者可以隨心所欲寫作，但若越界，就有禍了。</span>

#### 平等與自由的張力  
**Equality and Liberty**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Democratic communities have a natural taste for freedom: left to themselves they will seek it, cherish it, and view any deprivation of it with regret. But for equality their passion is ardent, insatiable, incessant, invincible: they call for equality in freedom; and if they cannot obtain that, they still call for equality in slavery.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">民主社會天生喜愛自由：若任其自然，他們會追求自由、珍惜自由，並對任何剝奪感到遺憾。但他們對平等的熱情卻熱烈、無止境、不斷且不可戰勝：他們要求在自由中實現平等；若得不到，他們仍會要求在奴役中實現平等。</span>

#### 民主與社會主義  
**Democracy and Socialism**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Democracy extends the sphere of individual freedom, socialism restricts it. Democracy attaches all possible value to each man; socialism makes each man a mere agent, a mere number. Democracy and socialism have nothing in common but one word: equality. But notice the difference: while democracy seeks equality in liberty, socialism seeks equality in restraint and servitude.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">民主擴大個人自由的範圍，社會主義則限制它。民主賦予每個人一切可能的價值；社會主義則使每個人成為單純的代理人、單純的數字。民主與社會主義除了一個詞「平等」之外，毫無共同之處。但請注意差別：民主在自由中尋求平等，社會主義則在約束與奴役中尋求平等。</span>

### 三、約翰·洛克  
**John Locke**

#### 自然權利  
**Natural Rights**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Being all equal and independent, no one ought to harm another in his life, health, liberty, or possessions.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">既然人人平等且獨立，任何人都不得傷害他人的生命、健康、自由或財產。</span>

#### 法律與自由  
**Law and Liberty**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">The end of law is not to abolish or restrain, but to preserve and enlarge freedom. Where there is no law, there is no freedom.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">法律的目的不是廢除或限制自由，而是保護並擴大自由。哪里沒有法律，哪里就沒有自由。</span>

#### 財產權的起源  
**The Origin of Property**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Every man has a property in his own person: this nobody has any right to but himself. The labour of his body, and the work of his hands, we may say, are properly his.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">每個人對自己的人身擁有財產權：除他自己以外，沒有人對此有任何權利。他身體的勞動，以及他雙手的工作，我們可以說，都正當地屬於他。</span>

### 四、孟德斯鳩  
**Montesquieu**

#### 權力的濫用  
**The Abuse of Power**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Constant experience shows us that every man invested with power is apt to abuse it, and to carry his authority as far as it will go.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">不斷的經驗告訴我們，每一個被賦予權力的人，都容易濫用權力，並把權力用到極限。</span>

#### 政治自由  
**Political Liberty**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Political liberty is to be found only in moderate governments; and even in these it is not always found. It is there only when there is no abuse of power. But constant experience shows us that every man invested with power is apt to abuse it... To prevent this abuse, it is necessary from the very nature of things that power should be a check to power.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">政治自由只存在於溫和的政府之中；即使在這些政府中，也並非總是存在。只有當權力不被濫用時，才有政治自由。但不斷的經驗告訴我們，每一個被賦予權力的人，都容易濫用權力……為了防止這種濫用，從事物的本質來看，必須以權力制約權力。</span>

#### 法律與正義的暴政  
**Tyranny under the Shield of Law**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">There is no greater tyranny than that which is perpetrated under the shield of the law and in the name of justice.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">沒有比打著法律與正義旗號所施行的暴政，更為嚴重的暴政了。</span>
