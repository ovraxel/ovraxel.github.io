# 奧威爾經典語錄精選

---

### 平等的終極悖論  
**The Ultimate Paradox of Equality**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">All animals are equal, but some animals are more equal than others.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">所有動物一律平等，但有些動物比其他動物更加平等。</span>

---

### 簡化的信條  
**The Simplified Maxim**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Four legs good, two legs bad.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">四條腿好，兩條腿壞。</span>

---

### 拿破崙永遠正確  
**Napoleon Is Always Right**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Napoleon is always right.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">拿破崙永遠正確。</span>

---

### Boxer 的座右銘  
**Boxer’s Motto**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">I will work harder.<br>Napoleon is always right.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">我要更加努力工作。<br>拿破崙永遠正確。</span>

---

### 老麥哲的革命宣言（節選）  
**Old Major’s Revolutionary Speech (Excerpt)**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Man is the only real enemy we have. Remove Man from the scene, and the root cause of hunger and overwork is abolished for ever.<br>…<br>All men are enemies. All animals are comrades.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">人類是我們唯一真正的敵人。把人類從我們的生活中消除，飢餓與過度勞累的根源就會永遠拔除。<br>……<br>所有的人都是敵人，所有的動物都是同志。</span>

---

### 革命的終章  
**The Final Scene of the Revolution**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">The creatures outside looked from pig to man, and from man to pig, and from pig to man again; but already it was impossible to say which was which.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">外面的動物從豬望向人，又從人望向豬，再從豬望向人；但已經分不清誰是豬，誰是人了。</span>

---

### 開篇第一句  
**The Opening Line**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">It was a bright cold day in April, and the clocks were striking thirteen.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">四月間，天氣寒冷晴朗，鐘敲了十三下。</span>

---

### 老大哥在看著你  
**Big Brother Is Watching You**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">BIG BROTHER IS WATCHING YOU</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">老大哥在看著你</span>

---

### 黨的三大口號  
**The Party’s Three Slogans**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">WAR IS PEACE<br>FREEDOM IS SLAVERY<br>IGNORANCE IS STRENGTH</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">戰爭即和平<br>自由即奴役<br>無知即力量</span>

---

### 控制過去與未來  
**Who Controls Past and Future**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Who controls the past controls the future. Who controls the present controls the past.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">誰控制過去就控制未來，誰控制現在就控制過去。</span>

---

### 自由的定義  
**The Definition of Freedom**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Freedom is the freedom to say that two plus two make four. If that is granted, all else follows.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">自由就是可以說二加二等於四的自由。如果承認這一點，其他一切就迎刃而解。</span>

---

### 未來的圖像  
**A Picture of the Future**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">If you want a picture of the future, imagine a boot stamping on a human face—forever.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">如果你想要一幅未來的圖像，想像一隻靴子永遠踩在一張人臉上。</span>

---

### 思想罪  
**Thoughtcrime**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Thoughtcrime does not entail death: thoughtcrime IS death.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">思想罪並不導致死亡：思想罪本身就是死亡。</span>

---

### 權力本身即目的  
**Power as an End in Itself**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Power is not a means; it is an end.<br>One does not establish a dictatorship in order to safeguard a revolution; one makes the revolution in order to establish the dictatorship.<br>The object of persecution is persecution. The object of torture is torture. The object of power is power.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">權力不是手段，而是目的。<br>建立獨裁不是為了保衛革命；進行革命是為了建立獨裁。<br>迫害的目的就是迫害，折磨的目的就是折磨，權力的目的就是權力。</span>

---

### 唯一屬於自己的東西  
**The Only Thing That Is Yours**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">Nothing was your own except the few cubic centimetres inside your skull.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">除了你頭蓋骨裡那幾立方厘米之外，沒有任何東西是屬於你自己的。</span>

---

### 希望在無產者身上  
**Hope Lies in the Proles**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">If there is hope, it lies in the proles.</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">如果有希望的話，希望在無產者身上。</span>

---

### 致未來與過去的問候  
**Greetings to the Future and the Past**

> <span style="color:#2E8B57; font-size:1.15em;">To the future or to the past, to a time when thought is free, when men are different from one another and do not live alone — to a time when truth exists and what is done cannot be undone: From the age of uniformity, from the age of solitude, from the age of Big Brother, from the age of doublethink — greetings!</span>

> <span style="color:#A8D5BA; font-size:1.1em;">致未來或過去，致一個思想自由的時代，致人們彼此不同卻不孤獨的時代——致真理尚存、已做之事無法抹滅的時代：來自千篇一律的時代，來自孤獨的時代，來自老大哥的時代，來自雙重思想的時代——向您致敬！</span>
