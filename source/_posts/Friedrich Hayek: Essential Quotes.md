# 海耶克經典語錄精選：來自《通往奴役之路》及其他著作

---

### 善意鋪成的地獄之路
**The Road to Hell is Paved with Good Intentions**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Is there a greater tragedy imaginable than that, in our endeavour consciously to shape our future in accordance with high ideals, we should in fact unwittingly produce the very opposite of what we have been striving for?</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">還有什麼悲劇比這更大？我們努力按照崇高的理想自覺地塑造未來，結果卻不知不覺地製造出與我們一直奮鬥的目標截然相反的東西。</span>

---

### 放棄自由換取保障
**Those Who Sacrifice Freedom for Security**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Those who would give up essential liberty to purchase a little temporary safety deserve neither liberty nor safety.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">那些願意放棄基本自由以換取一點暫時安全保障的人，既不配得到自由，也不配得到安全。</span>

---

### 金錢是最偉大的自由工具
**Money as the Greatest Instrument of Freedom**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Money is one of the greatest instruments of freedom ever invented by man. It is money which in existing society opens an astounding range of choice to the poor man, a range greater than that which not many generations ago was open to the wealthy.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">金錢是人類所發明的最偉大的自由工具之一。在現存社會中，只有金錢才向窮人開放一個驚人的選擇範圍——這個範圍比幾代以前向富人開放的範圍還要大。</span>

---

### 私有財產是自由的最重要保障
**Private Property as the Most Important Guarantee of Freedom**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">What our generation has forgotten is that the system of private property is the most important guarantee of freedom, not only for those who own property, but scarcely less for those who do not. It is only because the control of the means of production is divided among many people acting independently that nobody has complete power over us, that we as individuals can decide what to do with ourselves.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">我們這一代人已經忘記，私有財產制度是自由的最重要保障，這不僅對有產者如此，對無產者幾乎同樣重要。正是因為生產資料的控制權分散在許多獨立行動的人手中，才沒有任何人能對我們擁有完全的權力，我們作為個人才能決定自己要做什麼。</span>

---

### 緊急狀態總是侵蝕自由的藉口
**Emergencies as the Pretext for Eroding Liberty**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">‘Emergencies’ have always been the pretext on which the safeguards of individual liberty have been eroded.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">「緊急狀態」從來都是侵蝕個人自由保障的藉口。</span>

---

### 只服從法律才是自由
**Freedom Means Obedience Only to the Law**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">If a man is not free to follow his own plans and intentions, if he is subject to the arbitrary will of another, he is not free. The law is the only form in which the state can exercise its coercive power that is compatible with freedom. The purpose of the law is not to abolish or restrain, but to preserve and enlarge freedom.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">如果一個人不能自由地追隨自己的計畫與意圖，如果他服從於他人的任意意志，他就不是自由的。法律是國家行使強制權力時唯一與自由相容的形式。法律的目的不是廢除或限制自由，而是保護並擴大自由。如果一個人不需要服從任何人，只服從法律，那麼他就是自由的。</span>

---

### 經濟學的奇特任務
**The Curious Task of Economics**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">The curious task of economics is to demonstrate to men how little they really know about what they imagine they can design.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">經濟學的奇特任務，就是向人們證明：他們對自己想像能夠設計的事物，其實知道得何其之少。</span>

---

### 計劃越多，個人越難計劃
**The More the State Plans, the More Difficult Planning Becomes for the Individual**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">The more the state “plans,” the more difficult planning becomes for the individual.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">國家「計劃」得越多，個人就越難進行自己的計劃。</span>

---

### 哪裡沒有財產權，哪裡就沒有正義
**Where There Is No Property, There Is No Justice**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Where there is no property there is no justice.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">哪裡沒有財產權，哪裡就沒有正義。</span>

---

### 民主是手段而非目的
**Democracy Is a Means, Not an End**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">Democracy is essentially a means, a utilitarian device for safeguarding internal peace and individual freedom. As such it is by no means infallible or certain.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">民主本質上是一種手段，一種保障國內安定與個人自由的實用工具。它本身絕不是一貫正確或可靠無疑的。</span>

---

### 平等待人 vs 使人平等
**Treating People Equally vs Making Them Equal**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">There is all the difference in the world between treating people equally and attempting to make them equal. While the first is a condition of a free society, the second is merely a new form of servitude.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">平等待人與試圖使人平等，兩者之間有天壤之別。前者是自由社會的前提條件，後者則僅僅意味著一種新的奴役形式。</span>

---

### 政府成為唯一雇主時
**When the Government Becomes the Sole Employer**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">In a country where the sole employer is the State, opposition means death by slow starvation. The old principle, “He who does not work shall not eat,” has been replaced by a new one: “He who does not obey shall not eat.”</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">在一個政府是唯一雇主的國家裡，反抗就等於慢慢餓死。「不勞動者不得食」這個舊原則，已被「不服從者不得食」這個新原則所取代。</span>

---

### 使人間變成地獄的，往往是想把它變成天堂的想法
**What Turns a Country into Hell**

> <span style="color:#B7D9A8; font-size:1.15em;">The things that turn a country into a hell on earth are precisely those that “great men” try to turn into a paradise.</span>

> <span style="color:#E6D3A3; font-size:1.1em;">使一個國家變成人間地獄的東西，恰恰是那些「大人物」們總想試圖將其變成天堂的東西。</span>
